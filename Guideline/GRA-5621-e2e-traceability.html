Where the time goes

  • Shard 1 is always the long pole. Playwright 1.61.1 only shards by test count, so shard 1 collects the checkout and payment specs. Its 4 workers did 60–66% of the work they could have done; the other shards managed 81–93%.
  • PayPal checkout runs as a serial chain on one worker. All 16 tests in paypal-checkout.spec.ts (PLAORD-001 and PLAORD-002, 8 sites each) sit in one mode: 'default' describe. That runs them in order, because they authorise against one shared PayPal sandbox buyer. The chain took 20–49 min per OS per run.
  • Retries go to failures already explained. E2E-PLAORD-003 failed on every site in every run, even after retries. That is the Forter pre-auth decline (RCA 2026-10-29). It costs 28 test-min on macOS and 37 on Windows per run.
  • Five Retired duplicates still run (ERR-003, ERR-005, ERR-006, LOC-003, LOC-004), at 19 test-min on macOS and 37 on Windows per run.
  • The report job moves the raw results twice. It downloads every ui-test-results-* artifact only to check that the folder exists. It then re-uploads those results inside the "combined" report. Those two steps took 8–16 min of the 14–29 min job.
