Dr. Martens NZ: https://stag-drmartens-nz.accentgra.com/ ("Home page")		The homepage opens without an error page. The browser tab shows the title listed for the site, on both desktop and mobile. The page content is visible, starting with the large hero banner under the header.
2Look at the thin bar across the very top of the page, above the logo.NoneThe bar is visible and shows a promotional message, for example "Free express shipping on all orders over $150" on Platypus. The wording differs by site and campaign, and the message changes every few seconds.
3Watch the top bar until it has shown all of its messages, and look for the Qantas Points message.AU sites: Platypus AU, Skechers AU, Vans AU, Dr. Martens AU
NZ sites: Platypus NZ, Skechers NZ, Vans NZ, Dr. Martens NZ		On the AU sites, one message reads "Earn 2 Qantas Points per $1 spent*" next to the red Qantas logo, and it links to the site's Qantas page (the address ends in /qantas). Vans writes it in capitals or title case. The NZ sites never show a Qantas message.
4Compare the colours of the top bar, the header bar and the MENS or MEN menu link with the Expected colours table.Expected colours table: the Top bar, Header bar and MENS / MEN columns. Platypus and Skechers label the link MENS. Vans and Dr. Martens label it MEN.Each colour matches the brand's row. On Platypus, for example, the top bar is dark grey, the header bar is black and MENS is white. On Skechers the top bar is near-black, the header bar is white and MENS is dark grey. On mobile the menu links are hidden, so only the top bar and header bar are checked.
5Look at what the header bar contains.Search box hint text: "Find products, colours, fits..." on Platypus, and "What are you looking for?" on Skechers, Vans and Dr. Martens.On desktop, the logo sits on the left, followed by the menu links in a row (WOMEN, MEN, KIDS and so on), then the search box. The heart (wishlist), person (my account) and cart icons sit on the right. There is no three-line menu icon.
On mobile, the three-line menu icon sits on the left of the logo, and the heart, person and cart icons sit on the right. The menu links are hidden. The search box moves into its own full-width bar directly under the header.
6Scroll slowly down the page until you reach the footer.NoneThe top bar and the header bar stay fixed at the top of the screen all the way down. The header bar sits directly under the top bar with no gap. Neither bar hides or changes colour while you scroll.
7In the footer, find the "Contact Us" link and compare its text colour and the background behind it with the Expected colours table.Expected colours table: the "Contact Us" and Footer background columns.
Where the link is: Platypus under HELP & SUPPORT ("Contact us" on NZ), Skechers under Help & Support, Vans under MORE, and Dr. Martens under SUPPORT, written "CONTACT US".		Both colours match the brand's row. Skechers is the only brand whose footer background changes between desktop (pale blue-grey) and mobile (off-white).
