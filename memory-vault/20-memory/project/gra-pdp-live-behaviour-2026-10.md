Probed twice per site and viewport on 2026-10-31 with `.claude/skills/revise-xray-test-case/scripts/collectors/pdp.mjs` (men's listing, first shoe with colours and stock). The TEST-882 proposed steps in `Guideline/GRA-5621-e2e-traceability.html` are built on these facts.

- **Size system is a list, not gender toggle buttons.** "CHOOSE SIZE US MENS ⌄" (UK on Dr. Martens) opens US WOMENS / US MENS / EU / UK / CM (the set varies by product but always includes EU and CM). **The choice persists for the session**: after picking EU, a reload still shows EU. A test that switches it leaks into every later step in the same browser context. Contradicts the June note that Vans defaulted to CM, see [[pdp-004-005-vans-au-root-cause]].
- **Sold-out sizes are enabled buttons.** They are drawn crossed out, but have no `disabled` attribute; in-stock ones carry the class `available`. Picking a sold-out size shows "This item is currently sold out" and NOTIFY ME. **Fixed 2026-10-31:** `getAvailableSizes()` now also requires the `available` class (field `inStockSizeClass`). Before the fix it returned sold-out sizes, and `selectFirstPurchasableSize` gave up after 3; on the Vans Old Skool, sizes 3.5, 4 and 4.5 were all sold out. After the fix, PDP-005 and PDP-007 passed on all 8 sites in Chromium with no skips.
- **PDP-006-002 (Platypus NZ) fails on staging, and not because of that fix.** The first card on the men's listing is "PLAT CREW 3 PK BLK SZ 10-12" socks, which have no size buttons. `isSizeSelectorVisible()` still returns true via its "SIZE" text fallback (the disabled "Size Chart" button), so Add to Cart adds the socks with no validation, and the cart goes from 0 to 1. The test fails the same way on the old code.
- **Scanning products with `goBack()` is flaky on staging** (PDP-006 inline-loop attempt, 3 of 24 failed, 2026-10-31). On Skechers AU and NZ, Back returns to "All Men's" but the product grid never re-renders, so `waitForProductGrid()` times out after 60 s. On Platypus AU the page stayed on a PDP with the "Why did you decide to leave today?" exit survey open; the survey blocking Back is a suspicion, not proven. `findProductWithAvailableSizes()` also makes its first check before sizes render, so it can reject an in-stock product and go Back without needing to. The probe collector opens product links directly instead and had no navigation failures.
- **PDP-006 now opens products directly** with `EcommercePLPPage.getProductCardHrefs()` and `EcommercePDPPage.openProduct()`. That gave 0 Back-step failures in 48 runs, against 3 in 24 with `goBack()`. **Vans AU then failed in 3 of 6 runs.** Each time, the "Join The Crew" Bloomreach pop-up was open over the page, so `addToCart()`'s forced click never reached the button and no validation appeared. Opening products by clicking a card had passed Vans AU 5 of 5 times. One explanation is that full page loads re-arm the pop-up, but that is unproven. Calling `ensureNoOverlay()` again right before the click did not help, and I reverted it. Unverified leads:
  - **Forced click:** `force: true` skips the actionability wait, which is when the `addLocatorHandler` from `setupBloomreachHandler()` fires.
  - **Close button:** if it sits outside the container that `dismissAcquisitionPopup()` searches, the method falls back to Escape, which doesn't close this pop-up.
- **Pop-up root cause, confirmed from the failure snapshot:** `dialog "Join The Crew Get 10% Off Your First Order."` and its `button "Close popup"` sit outside `[class*="bloomreach-acquisition-popup"][class*="state-open"]`. **Fix (2026-10-31):** `dismissAcquisitionPopup()` now clicks that dialog's Close button first, using the same name pattern as `account-modal.ts`, and `addToCart()` calls it right before its forced click. Afterwards Vans AU PDP-006 passed 3 of 3, then failed 1 of 1 in the broad run with the pop-up already closed (only the "Join Us" side tab showing). The cause of that last failure is unknown, so treat it as improved but still flaky.
- **Broad run, 2026-10-31 22:28 to 22:58** (PDP, cart and error-handling smoke plus INT-001, Chromium): 154 passed, 21 failed, 1 skipped.
  - **Platypus AU, 12 tests:** every test that scans with `goBack()` failed (CART-002 to 010, INT-001-001, ERR-003, PDP-005, PDP-007). All are stuck on "CHUCK TAYLOR ALL STAR LIFT OX", which has no size buttons, with the exit survey open after Back. That product is now first on the WOMENS listing. In the same run PDP-006, which opens product links directly, passed on Platypus AU. Every one of these failures shows the survey open, but that the survey blocks Back is still unproven.
  - **Not attributable to these changes:** ERR-001 (404 brand name, 6 sites) and ERR-005 (Dr. Martens AU wrong password) use no page object that was changed, and neither was baselined. INT-001-002 (Platypus NZ, `cart_add` missing) does go through the scan and `addToCart()`, and wasn't investigated.
- **Scan now opens product links directly (2026-10-31 23:05).** `findProductWithAvailableSizes()` in `smoke-helpers.ts` opens each product with `getProductCardHrefs()` + `openProduct()` and waits for sizes on every product. PDP-005/006/007 now call it instead of running their own Back-based loops.
  - **PDP-005/006/007:** 72 of 72 passed (8 sites, 3 repeats).
  - **Cart + error-handling + INT-001:** 111 passed, 8 failed, 1 skipped. All 12 Platypus AU scan failures are gone. The remaining failures are ERR-001 on 6 sites and INT-001-002, the same as before, plus CART-010-005 on Vans AU. That one showed "Size was not chosen" with no size selected and no pop-up open, then passed 3 of 3 when rerun, so it looks intermittent.
- **Still using Back:** PDP-002's inline loop and `findPdpWithColourSwatches()` (PDP-003). Both pass today because the first product has two colours. `checkout-address-prefill.spec.ts` (excluded from the Chromium project) has its own Back loop. The checkout regression specs were not run, because they place orders.
- **Afterpay** is `<square-placement>` with an open shadow root. The text "or 4 payments of $X with" appears about 10 s after load, where X = price ÷ 4 rounded to the cent. It shows on all 8 sites.
- **Add to Cart:** two buttons on mobile, a fixed bottom bar (off screen until you scroll, on Platypus and Vans) and the inline one. Recommendation carousels add "+ Add To Cart" buttons, so match the label exactly. With no size chosen, "Size was not chosen" appears on all 8 sites. After adding, the cart panel shows "YOUR CART" / "Added to Cart" / "{Colour} | {size} US Mens" ("UK" on Dr. Martens), and "Continue Shopping" closes it.
- **Desktop gallery:** a grid of large photos on Platypus, Vans and Dr. Martens. Skechers shows one photo with arrows and a thumbnail strip.
- **Probe gotcha:** the Vans AU mobile "Join The Crew" pop-up reopens on a timer. Dismiss it again right before each tap.
- **Staging data bug (unreported):** on Platypus NZ, the Vans Old Skool `/old-skool-vn-0d3hbka-bbk.html` has the tab title "Shop Asics OLD SKOOL…".
